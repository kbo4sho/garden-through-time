## Craft

Overlapping UV cluster cards replace high-vert leaf ribbons. A tuft mask (irregular ellipse, hard discard — no hashed dither) hides the card rectangle. Deciduous winter spray cards live on the branch layer and key off `_phase > 1`. `plantState` is still the only calendar.

## What a critic should still look at

Photographic frames still have finer twig/leaf grain and a softer overall silhouette. Attempt 2 reads as overlapping tuft masses rather than attempt 1’s discrete leaf ribbons. Whether that is “as rich as the photo frames” is for YRI, not the builder.
